FBA — Bikash Thakur portfolio

Live website: https://mrinal505.github.io/fba/

Open index.html in your browser to view the website locally. Keep the images,
stylesheets, and JavaScript beside index.html. No installation or build needed.

Files:
- index.html: page text, social links, portfolio, and featured reel selections
- style.css: core layout and mobile styling
- depth.css: 3D transforms, rotating rings, reel gallery, and video dialog
- app.js: motion controls, pointer tilt, carousel navigation, video dialog
- *.jpg: portfolio and reel cover images from the creator's Instagram profile

Reels load through Instagram embeds only after the visitor presses play.
Instagram controls playback and may require sign-in. The Watch on Instagram
link always opens the original reel. Video files are not included.

To replace a featured reel, update its data-reel shortcode, data-title,
accessible label, image, and visible text in index.html.

Mobile support includes touch scrolling, large buttons, no autoplay downloads,
and reduced-motion preferences. Pause motion stops the decorative animation.
